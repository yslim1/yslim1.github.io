I am a postdoctoral research fellow at the Research Institute of Advanced Materials (RIAM), Seoul National University, working with Prof. Seungwu Han. My research develops data-driven and AI-enabled approaches for materials discovery and atomistic simulation, with a focus on porous and inorganic materials.

My goal is to bridge theory and experiment by building predictive atomistic models and computational methods for complex materials problems. I combine first-principles and molecular simulation methods—including density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics (MD), and grand canonical Monte Carlo (GCMC)—with machine-learning interatomic potentials and large language models.

Research interests
======

- Machine-learning interatomic potentials
- AI-accelerated materials discovery and crystal structure prediction
- Metal-organic and covalent-organic frameworks
- Carbon capture, gas storage, and separation
- Atomistic modeling across simulation and process scales

Current research
======

- Benchmarking universal machine-learning interatomic potentials, including SevenNet, MACE, eSEN, and UMA
- Accelerating crystal structure prediction for unsolved materials
- Developing high-fidelity computational workflows for materials discovery
